Slogan

Paws up for playtime!

The slogan captures the excitement and joy. “Paws up” symbolizes the enthusiasm, while “for playtime” focuses on the core of our service. It aligns with our mission to create a playful, trustworthy community where dogs and dog owners can connect with playdates, care and friendship for their furry companions.

Brand message

Making it easier for dog lovers to find playmates for their pups

The brand message reflects the values by making it easy for dog owners to connect, promoting playfulness and companionship, while still ensuring convenience and peace of mind through safe and reliable connections

Vision

To connect dog owners with trusted like-minded dog lovers, ensuring every dog gets the attention, play, and care they deserve.

Our vision focuses on connecting the dog owners with like-minded pet lovers, ensuring that they will get their attention and play they deserve, while fostering trusted community based on companionship and care beyond basic needs

Mission

Our mission is to create a vibrant and trusted community where dog owners can connect, socialize, and find pawmates, care, and friendship for their furry companions. We make every dog's life happier and healthier by fostering meaningful connections and offering convenient services like playdates and dog sitting. Because every pup deserves a fur-ever friend!

This mission supports the need for a trusted, social space where dog owners can easily connect and care for their pets. By offering services like playdates and dog sitting, it promotes happier, healthier lives for dogs while building a strong, supportive community. Every pup deserves friendship, and so do their humans.

User Interface Patterns

On our homepage (index page), we use different design elements to make the website easy and fun to use. At the top of the page, there is a video that grabs your attention right away. This video gives you a quick idea of what our website is about and encourages you to scroll down and explore more.

Our header and footer are always visible on every page. These sections help you navigate through the website quickly by giving direct links to other pages like the contact page, services, and more.

Contact Page

Our contact page is simple and easy to use. You can type in your questions, feedback, or anything else you want to ask us. It's a great way to get in touch if you need help or want more information. We make sure to respond as soon as possible to help you out.

Service Page

On the service page, we use cards to display our different services. Each card has a title, a short description, and sometimes a fun image or icon. We wanted to show who we are in a way that feels personal, fun, and playful. That’s why we chose an interactive design with movement and clear structure. Each card also includes a button that redirects to our LinkedIn profiles, making it easier for people to connect with us.

How Does Website Design Fit the Values and Personality of the Brand?

Our website is fully based on the needs and preferences of our target audience, discovered through research. Many existing dog-related websites often use basic or neutral colors and have a more traditional, simple look. While that works for some, we felt it didn’t match the energy we want to bring across.

Our brand is all about being playful, fun, and full of color — just like the dogs (and people!) we’re designing for. We brought that vibe into every part of the website, from the bright and cheerful colors to the fonts and tone of the text. The colors pop off the screen, which helps grab attention and makes the site more enjoyable to explore. We know from our research that our audience enjoys vibrant and positive designs, and we wanted to reflect that in a way that feels natural and exciting.

Every element was chosen with care to reflect who we are as a brand — creative, friendly, and full of energy.

How Does Website Design Fit the Marketing and Communication Strategy?

While designing our website, we mainly focused on consistency and simplicity. We wanted to make sure that our target audience can easily understand and navigate the site without getting confused or overwhelmed. That’s why we kept the structure clean and straightforward.

We avoided adding too many extra pages or complicated features, ensuring everything is easy to scroll through and visually clear. Information is grouped in a way that makes sense, and familiar UX patterns are used to support the user journey.

Our goal was to create a website that looks playful and fun, but still functions in a very clear and simple way.

How Does Website Design Help Showcase the Unique Value Proposition of the Product?

Just like the rest of the website, we ensured that every page is clear and easy to understand for our target audience. Consistency was key — the layout, colors, fonts, and overall style are the same across all pages to create a seamless experience.

We focused on making our product stand out with visual clarity and smart placement. Users don't need to search for key information — it's easy to find and visually emphasized while still fitting into the playful and colorful design.

Our website reflects the core values of our brand and product: fun, ease, and a strong connection between people and their dogs.

    This testing protocol is designed to guide participants through the process of evaluating the Happy Tails website. The aim is to ensure that the website is intuitive, visually appealing, and supports a smooth user experience. Additionally, we want to verify that users understand the website's core offerings and can easily access key information.

    We will test with 6 participants who meet the following criteria:

    • Aged between 30 and 50 years old
    • Have basic knowledge of using websites and digital tools
    • Reside in various parts of the Netherlands

    All the testing will be conducted in-person at home or on another remote location.

    Procedure

    • Introduction: Brief the participant on the test and its purpose.
    • Task Walkthrough: Provide a written list of the tasks to complete.

    • Observation: The team will observe user behavior and note any issues or confusion.
    • Post-Test Interview: Conduct a short debrief with the participant based on the questions above.

Context of campaign and promotional activities

Welcome to “Happy Tails”! A website platform where dog owners can easily arrange playdates, build a sense of community, and create enriching social experiences for their pets. Have limited time on your hands? No worries! Happy Tails provides convenient dog walking, dog sitting, and playmate services that can be as simple as pick-ups at your door. Want to have a dog but travel a lot? We gotcha! Happy Tails also gives you the opportunity to take care of dogs as a hobby securely.

The Paw’s Up for Playtime! campaign aims to promote dog playdates, foster community engagement, and drive more traffic to our website and platforms. Our target audience consists of dog lovers and enthusiasts between the ages of 30 and 50. The campaign will primarily run on Instagram, Facebook, and through physical flyers to maximize reach and engagement.

For social media content, we will use the hashtags #HappyTails, #PlayMore, and #HealthyTails to create consistency across platforms. The posting schedule includes four posts per week per platform, two daily stories, and one interactive story per week, featuring polls and Q&A sessions to boost audience interaction.

These key messages reflect our core purpose: bringing dogs and owners together in a safe and fun environment. By emphasizing socialization, well-being, and community, we ensure that every dog has the chance to enjoy life to the fullest while their owners build meaningful connections too.

We chose Instagram and Facebook because they align with the digital habits of our target demographic: dog owners aged 30–50 who are tech-savvy and engaged in social media communities. Instagram offers strong visual content that allows us to showcase the joyful and social aspects of Happy Tails through photos, reels, and stories, formats that resonate with a lifestyle-focused audience. It’s also where users tend to follow pet-related pages and engage with uplifting, pet-friendly content.

Facebook, on the other hand, is ideal for creating a community-centered environment. Many older millennials and Gen X users still prefer Facebook for group interactions, event planning, and shared posts. This aligns well with our goal of creating a trust-based, supportive dog care network. Despite very minimal growth on Facebook, it has potential to become a key platform for fostering long-form engagement and targeted local outreach.

Our content was posted 5-6 times a week across both platforms, balancing a mix of reels, static posts, and stories. This schedule was selected based on insights into when our audience is most active: evenings and weekends, when pet owners are likely unwinding or looking for plans with their dogs (Social Petworker, 2024). Posting regularly throughout the week allows us to maintain visibility in our followers’ feeds without overwhelming them. Consistency also supports brand recognition, as reiterated in Pinckney Harmon’s (2020) point about how repeated visuals enhance brand familiarity. Over time, this rhythm helps to build trust and a reliable presence in the digital space.

The most used social media platforms for pet brands are facebook, Tiktok and Instagram (Andrews). Because our target group is between 30 and 50, their most used social media accounts are Youtube, Facebook and instagram (Sheikh). That's why we chose only to post on Instagram and facebook. The best times to post on a day are in the earliest of the morning, or after working hours (Lang). We chose to post after working hours because our age gap is working and is spending less time on their phone.

We want to post 4 times a week. In the first week will be the launch posts of our website to get the audience excited. Those posts will be on instagram and facebook. Throughout those posts we will also make some stories on instagram to boost our interaction with the audience. After the first week we are happily showing how the users are enjoying our services and share the experiences. The reason why we want to tell and show the stories is to attract more users and followers. We also want to introduce rubrics that we will use throughout the full lifespan of our socials. We want to create a recognisable and consistent content road map for our followers so they know what to expect

One of the biggest takeaways from our social media campaign was that cross-posting the same content on Instagram and Facebook doesn’t lead to the same results. While our Instagram page steadily grew in reach and engagement, Facebook consistently underperformed. This taught us that even if platforms seem similar, the way users interact with content can vary significantly. In hindsight, we should have tailored our Facebook posts more by using a more community-focused tone, including longer captions, or sharing relevant articles and dog-owner resources that perform better with Facebook's older demographic.

We also realized that while our posting schedule was consistent, it might not have been optimized for when our audience was actually online. Our Week 3 engagement showed a noticeable dip, which could have been a sign of posting fatigue or misaligned timing. In the future, we would use platform insights more effectively to identify high-traffic windows and experiment with posting times to maximize visibility.

Another thing we learned is that interactivity is key. While our visuals were on brand and informative, we could have created more space for followers to participate, polls, Q and A stickers, giveaways, or even a consistent “dog of the week” shoutout would have added more community involvement. These features are especially important when trying to grow a loyal and interactive audience.

Lastly, although we used hashtags, we now understand how much of a difference the right hashtags make. Our set was too generic, which limited our discoverability. In hindsight, we would research more targeted and location-specific hashtags to connect with local dog owners and niche communities who are more likely to engage and convert into users of our platform.

Overall, the project taught us the importance of platform-specific content, audience timing, and interactive storytelling. Going forward, we’d approach social media not just as a place to inform, but a space to spark meaningful conversations and community.

Management

Lean Canvas

    Problem

  • Many dog owners struggle to connect with others in their neighborhood. Balancing work and pet care can be challenging, often leading to fewer social interactions for both owner and dog, especially in single-dog households where stimulation is limited.
  • Finding suitable playmates is another issue. Owners may not know others open to dog interactions, especially if they have larger dogs, which can sometimes be seen as intimidating.
  • Time constraints also play a role. Our target audience (ages 30–50) often works full-time, and short walks may not provide enough exercise or stimulation for their pets.
  • Lack of trustworthy solutions: Finding trustworthy solutions and services is difficult. Nobody wants to hand out their dog to some stranger, and finding the people who will take care and love to spend time with your dog is hard. Especially in small villages.

Solution

  • Happy Tails helps dog owners connect with others nearby for walks and playdates. Our platform makes it easy to let your dog play while you relax, chat, and form real connections with fellow dog lovers.
  • With simple features and optional services, Happy Tails removes the stress of finding playmates. You can easily discover and message local users, no more awkward approaches or uncertainty.
  • To ensure that all our users are real we include a verification and review system. Making sure that ther dog is in safe hands by a trusted person. Reading good or bad reviews will benefit both groups

Customer Segments

  • Our target audience consists of dog owners aged 30 to 50 who want to socialize their pets while connecting with like-minded individuals. Many owners seek playmates for their dogs and opportunities to expand their own social circles through shared pet experiences. Additionally, a significant number are interested in flexible and reliable dog-sitting services to balance their busy schedules.
  • Our primary audience includes young professionals, families, remote workers, and single individuals who integrate their dogs into daily life. They prioritize their pet’s well-being, enjoy outdoor activities, and often need trusted pet care solutions, especially when traveling. Many live in urban or suburban areas with limited open spaces, making scheduled playdates a valuable alternative. Expats and newcomers also benefit from a community-driven platform to build a trusted pet-care network.
  • Dog owners in this group are comfortable using digital platforms to arrange meetups and value cost-effective, reliable alternatives to expensive pet care services. By addressing their needs, such as finding playmates, reducing guilt about leaving their dogs alone, and creating a supportive community, we are building more than just a service. Happy Tails is a trusted space where dogs can play, owners can connect, and reliable pet care is always within reach.
  • We want to focus on the Netherlands first. We can put all our time and effort to make our brand appealing and well-known to this audience. We can build a solid reputation within the country. After having a bigger target audience, we can enlarge and scale up to other countries.

Revenue streams

  • At Happy Tails, we generate revenue primarily through sponsorships from veterinarians and local trainers. These partnerships are vital for funding our day-to-day operations and providing the necessary resources to keep the platform running. By partnering with professionals who align with our values, we not only receive financial support but also gain credibility in the pet care community. This relationship allows us to offer high-quality content and services to dog owners while ensuring that our platform remains sustainable. 1 year estimate: 3 partners x €200/month x 12 months = €7,200/year
  • We organize a variety of events to raise funds and expand our brand presence. These events, which include entry fees, raffles, and limited-edition merchandise sales, serve as a great way to engage the community while generating revenue. The limited-edition merchandise, ranging from apparel to pet accessories, helps us further promote Happy Tails and create a sense of exclusivity. These events not only bring in funds but also allow us to build a stronger connection with our community of dog owners, ensuring that our mission continues to grow. year estimate: 12 events x €300 average revenue per event = €3,600/year 1 year estimate: 10 pieces of merchandise sold x €25 x 12 events = €3,000/year
  • To diversify our revenue, we’ve also established affiliate partnerships with reputable pet supply brands. These partnerships offer a win-win situation, as we earn a commission when users make purchases through our referral links. This income stream enables us to continue offering valuable services to dog owners, all while providing them with access to quality pet products that can benefit both them and their dogs. We ensure that the products we recommend align with our values and are trusted by the community. 1 year estimate: 100 purchases/month x €2 commission x 12 months = €2,400/year
  • We seek corporate sponsorships from pet-friendly businesses that share our commitment to supporting dog owners and promoting a dog-friendly lifestyle. By offering visibility within our platform and events, we provide these businesses with the opportunity to reach a targeted audience of dog owners and pet enthusiasts. In return, these businesses contribute financially to help us continue our work. This mutual relationship not only supports our mission but also helps pet-friendly brands grow alongside Happy Tails. 1 year estimate: 4 sponsors x €750 = €3,000/year. Total 1 year estimate: €25.500/year
  • When people are using our service, they will make a payment between each other. Because we provide the service, the safety and the community, we will get a small fee from the service This fee will be around 7%. This fee is needed to maintain our services and socials. We are also focussing on more features and a better user experience. 1 year estimate: 500 monthly transactions x €15 average value x 12 months x 7% fee = €6,300/year

    Services/products

  • Our product, Happy Tails, is a platform designed to connect dog owners with one another to facilitate playdates, socialization, and reliable pet care. We offer a space for dog owners to find trustworthy playmates for their dogs, join a community of like-minded individuals, and arrange flexible, affordable care options such as dog sitting.
  • This service contributes to our unique value proposition by offering a hassle-free way for dog owners to meet others nearby while ensuring their dogs get the socialization and care they need. Unlike other services that focus purely on dog sitting or walking, we emphasize community-building and making connections, allowing both dogs and owners to create lasting relationships.
  • The fit between Happy Tails and our brand identity is clear. Our brand is centered around fostering connections, trust, and a sense of community. We are committed to making the lives of dogs and their owners more social, joyful, and stress-free. By focusing on creating an easy way for dog owners to meet each other and find support within their local areas, we align with the values of socialization, companionship, and care that our brand represents.

Validation of Assumptions

1. Assumption: Dog owners struggle to find local playmates for their dogs. We based this on the conversations with dog owners around us. Many said they didn’t know other owners nearby or weren’t sure if people were open to dog playdates, especially for larger dogs. This showed a clear need for a platform that makes those connections easier.

2. Assumption: Dog owners between 30–50 are open to using digital platforms. We shared our concept through Instagram and got positive reactions, especially from people in that age range. Flyers with QR codes also worked well, showing that our audience is digitally active and willing to try new solutions.

Reflection on the Trademark “Happy Tails”

At first, our idea was to make a dog food website with healthy options and reviews. But we realized that emotional connection and community were stronger needs among dog owners. That insight led to our pivot toward social dog playdates.

We chose the name Happy Tails because it’s warm, memorable, and reflects joy, connection, and wagging tails, everything our platform stands for. Compared to names like PawPals or DogGo, Happy Tails felt more unique and emotionally fitting.
